## Document 1: Standard Beverage Recipes

**Title**: Regular Beverage Preparation Standards

- **Americano**: Double shot espresso (about 36ml) + hot water to 240ml (water temperature 92°C).
- **Latte**: Double shot espresso + steamed whole milk 200ml, milk foam thickness ≤0.5cm.
- **Cappuccino**: Single shot espresso + steamed milk 150ml, thicker foam (about 1–1.5cm), can sprinkle cinnamon powder on top.
- **Mocha**: Single shot espresso + chocolate sauce 15g + steamed milk 180ml, topped with whipped cream (dine-in only).
- **Iced Americano**: Double shot espresso + ice cubes + cold water to fill cup (about 300ml).

> Note: All espresso shots use the shop's standard Italian coffee machine, grind size set to "medium-fine", extraction time 25-30 seconds.

## Document 11: Pour-Over Coffee Operation Manual

**Title**: V60 Pour-Over Standard Process (for Single-Origin Beans)

1. **Preparation**:
   - Coffee beans: 15g (medium-light roast), grind size ≈ white sugar
   - Water temperature: 90–93°C, total water volume: 250ml
2. **Steps**:
   - Bloom: Pour 30ml water, wait 30 seconds (observe degassing)
   - First circle: Center to outside to center to 120ml (45 seconds)
   - Second circle: Slowly pour to 250ml (total time 1:45±10 seconds)
3. **Serving**: Remove dripper, gently swirl server to even out concentration.

> Note: Ethiopian Yirgacheffe recommends 90°C water temperature to avoid bitterness.

## Document 28: Milk Steaming Standard Illustration (Text Description Version)

**Title**: Ideal Milk Foam State Description

- **Temperature**: 60-65℃ (feels hot but can touch briefly)
- **Texture**:
  - Smooth like "wet paint", no large bubbles
  - Forms continuous thin stream when pouring, can do latte art
- **Failure Examples**:
  - ❌ Temperature too high (>70℃): Milk scorched, sweetness lost
  - ❌ Under-steamed: Liquid thin, cannot blend with espresso
  - ❌ Over-steamed: Foam dry and stiff, like shaving cream

> New employees must practice at least 20 cups before independent operation.

## Document 30: Customer Common Misunderstandings Clarification

**Title**: 5 Questions That Need Proactive Explanation

1. **"Why is oat milk more expensive than milk?"**
   → Because plant-based ingredients have higher costs and shorter shelf life.

2. **"Is espresso more energizing?"**
   → Single shot espresso has about 63mg caffeine, Americano has more total caffeine due to more water (about 120mg).

3. **"Does sugar-free mean completely no sugar?"**
   → Yes, but milk/oat milk naturally contains lactose (about 5g/200ml).

4. **"Why does pour-over take so long?"**
   → Because precise control of water temperature and pouring speed is needed to ensure balanced flavor extraction.

5. **"Why charge packaging fee for bringing own cup?"**
   → We don't! Bring your own cup gets ¥3 discount, and no packaging needed.
