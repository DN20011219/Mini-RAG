## Document 1: Standard Beverage Recipes (v3.2)

**Title**: Regular Beverage Preparation Standards (Including Tolerance, Alternatives and Customization Guide)

### Americano
- **Standard Recipe**: Double shot espresso (about 36ml, tolerance ±2ml) + hot water to 240ml (water temperature strictly controlled at 92±1°C).
- **Why 92°C?**: Too high will extract bitter taste, too low will be sour; shop's constant temperature water heater has preset this value, do not adjust manually.
- **Customization Options**:
  - Less water: Can make to 200ml (must note "less water Americano" in POS)
  - Iced Americano variant: See "Iced Americano" entry, cannot mix with hot water
- **Common Mistakes**:
  - ❌ Add water first then pour espresso → Causes crema to dissipate, flavor becomes flat
  - ✅ Correct order: Extract espresso first, then slowly inject hot water

### Latte
- **Standard Recipe**: Double shot espresso + steamed whole milk 200ml (milk foam thickness ≤0.5cm, measure with gauge).
- **Milk Requirements**: Must use "Local Dairy" whole milk (fat content ≥3.5%), skim milk prohibited for lattes (cannot form stable foam).
- **Steaming Technique**:
  - Insert steam wand 1cm below liquid surface, first "hiss" air intake for 1 second, then submerge to heat
  - Stop when temperature reaches 60°C (feels hot but can hold for 3 seconds)
- **Visual Acceptance**:
  - Surface smooth as mirror after pouring, no large bubbles
  - If used for latte art, must complete within 30 seconds (otherwise foam collapses)
- **Exceptions**:
  - Customer requests "thick foam latte" → Should recommend cappuccino instead, and explain difference

### Cappuccino
- **Standard Recipe**: Single shot espresso (18ml) + steamed milk 150ml, milk foam thickness 1.0–1.5cm (can use special measuring spoon to test).
- **Why thicker foam?**: Traditional Italian ratio is 1:1:1 (espresso:liquid milk:milk foam), our shop adjusted to 1:0.8:0.7 to suit local taste.
- **Decoration Standards**:
  - Can sprinkle cinnamon powder (dine-in only, takeaway easy to spill)
  - Prohibited to use chocolate powder (belongs to mocha series)
- **Customer Misunderstanding Response**:
  > Q: "Why less foam than the shop next door?"
  > A: "We pursue dense milk foam rather than fluffy, this makes smoother texture, would you like to try?"

### Mocha
- **Standard Recipe**: Single shot espresso + chocolate sauce 15g (calibrated with electronic scale, tolerance ±1g) + steamed milk 180ml, topped with whipped cream (dine-in only).
- **Chocolate Sauce Management**:
  - Refrigerate after opening, shelf life 7 days (label notes opening date)
  - Winter needs to be taken out 10 minutes early to warm up, otherwise difficult to pump
- **Whipped Cream Operation**:
  - Use N₂O gas whip cream gun, pressure 0.8MPa
  - Squeeze height 2cm, in spiral shape
  - Takeaway orders automatically replaced with "extra chocolate sauce 5g", and noted on receipt "no cream (takeaway)"
- **Allergy Notice**:
  - Chocolate sauce contains soy lecithin, those allergic to soy need to be informed in advance

### Iced Americano
- **Standard Recipe**: Double shot espresso + ice cubes 120g (about 8 standard cubes) + cold water to fill cup (total capacity about 300ml).
- **Ice Cube Standards**:
  - Use ice maker "clear ice" mode (not crushed ice)
  - Morning shift checks ice bucket cleanliness daily, discard immediately if mold spots
- **Dilution Control**:
  - Espresso needs to be poured directly over ice (not add water first), use ice melting to control concentration
  - If customer requests "less ice", reduce ice to 80g, supplement water to 300ml
- **Summer Special Notice** (June-August):
  - Can provide "double shot iced Americano" (+¥3), to cope with accelerated dilution at high temperature

> **General Note**: All espresso shots use shop's La Marzocco Linea Mini Italian coffee machine, grind size set to "medium-fine" (scale 4.5/10), extraction time 25-30 seconds. If environmental humidity >70%, grind size needs to be adjusted finer by 0.5 notch (recorded in "Daily Parameter Table").

**Title**: Complete Process for Working Hours, Leave, Shift Changes and Exception Handling

- **Standard Business**: Daily 7:00 – 20:00 (open year-round, except Spring Festival Day 1)

  - Format: `3/24(Mon): morning, evening | 3/25(Tue): morning`

  - Personal leave ≤2 days per month, exceeding requires store manager special approval
- **Emergency Situations** (e.g., immediate family critically ill):

## Document 3: Equipment Maintenance Log Template (v4.1)

**Title**: Coffee Machine, Grinder, Water Filter System Full Cycle Maintenance Manual

### Daily Operations (Morning/Evening Shift Each Execute Once)
- **Before Opening** (Morning Shift):
  1. Empty drip tray (weigh and record, >200g needs report)
  2. Flush group head 3 times (5 seconds each, observe water flow uniformity)
  3. Check boiler pressure gauge (0.8-1.0 bar is normal)
- **Every 4 Hours** (All Shifts):
  - Use blind filter + Cafiza cleaning powder for backflushing
  - Steps: Install blind filter → Add 1 scoop powder → Extract 10 seconds → Stop 5 seconds → Repeat 3 times → Rinse with clean water 2 times
  - Record in "Backflushing Log", including operator, time, abnormal descriptions
- **After Closing** (Evening Shift):
  1. Drain boiler remaining water (open steam valve 10 seconds)
  2. Turn off main power (not standby button)
  3. Clean grinder:
     - Empty bean hopper, use soft brush to clean burr residual powder
     - Run Grindz special cleaning beans for 30 seconds (execute every Monday, Thursday)

### Weekly Maintenance (Store Manager Executes, Sunday Evening Shift)
- **Water Filter Cartridge**:
  - Model: 3M Aqua-Pure AP11T
  - Replacement cycle: Every 4 weeks (or flow meter reaches 5000L)
  - Record: Take photo of old cartridge for archive, enter new cartridge serial number into system
- **Steam Wand Unclogging**:
  - Use 0.7mm special needle to gently poke from outlet inward (do not use paper clips!)
  - Test: Empty steam spray 3 seconds, observe if forms straight line (not scattered)
- **Grinder Calibration**:
  - Test extraction time with 18g coffee powder (target 28 seconds)
  - If deviation >2 seconds, fine-tune grind size

### Troubleshooting Process
1. Preliminary inspection:
   - No pressure? → Check water source valve
   - Slow extraction? → Clean group head
2. Still ineffective → Send photo/video to Engineer Zhang via WeChat
3. Engineer Zhang response:
   - Arrives within 2 hours on workdays
   - Non-workdays charge ¥200 expedite fee
4. During repair:
   - Use backup Aeropress to make Americano/Latte (limit 5 cups/hour)

## Document 4: Customer Complaint Handling Guide (v5.0)

**Title**: Service Issue Response, Compensation Standards and Legal Boundaries

### Four-Step Method (Must Execute in Order)
1. **Listen**:
   - Maintain eye contact, lean forward 15 degrees
   - Do not interrupt, do not argue, respond with "mm" "I understand"
   - Duration ≤3 minutes, escalate to store manager if exceeds
2. **Apologize**:
   - Standard script: "We're very sorry for the inconvenience!" (even if not our fault)
   - Prohibited: "But..." "Because..." "Others also..."
3. **Resolve** (by issue type):
   - **Wrong Drink**:
     - Immediately remake (highest priority)
     - Take back original drink for disposal (cannot reuse)
     - Give small cookies (cost ¥0.8/piece)
   - **Long Wait** (>15 minutes):
     - Give "next drink 10% off coupon" (system generated, valid 7 days)
     - If customer refuses, can upgrade to "free small Americano"
   - **Hygiene Issue** (e.g., cup stains):
     - Store manager appears, full refund
     - Give "apology coupon" (for next use only, cannot exchange for cash)
     - Submit improvement report within 24 hours
4. **Record**:
   - Fill out "Customer Complaint Registration Form" (paper + electronic)
   - Fields: Time, customer characteristics, issue description, handler, compensation method, follow-up improvements
   - Store manager reviews and archives within 24 hours

### Compensation Limits and Authority
| Issue Type | Employee Can Decide | Requires Store Manager Approval |
|------------|---------------------|--------------------------------|
| Drink Remake | ✓ | — |
| Give Cookies | ✓ | — |
| 10% Off Coupon | ✓ | — |
| Full Refund | — | ✓ |
| Give Regular-Priced Drink | — | ✓ |

### Legal Red Lines
- **Never Promise**: "Next time definitely..." "Absolutely won't happen again..."
- **Never Admit**: "Our hygiene is substandard" → Change to "We will strengthen inspections"
- **Video Retrieval**: Only store manager can apply to view surveillance, and requires customer written consent

## Document 11: Pour-Over Coffee Operation Manual (v4.0)

**Title**: V60 Pour-Over Standardization (Including Bean Adaptation and Complaint Response)

### General Process (15g : 250ml)
1. **Preparation**:
   - Filter paper: rinsed with hot water (remove paper taste, preheat pot)
   - Water temperature: 90-93℃ (fine-tune by bean type)
   - Grind: White sugar texture (Baratza Encore scale 18)
2. **Pouring**:
   - Bloom: 30ml, 30 seconds (observe bubble uniformity)
   - First circle: Center→outside→inside, to 120ml (45 seconds)
   - Second circle: Slow spiral pour to 250ml (total time 105±10 seconds)
3. **Serving**:
   - Remove dripper, gently swirl server 3 times (concentration uniform)
   - Pour into preheated ceramic cup (cup temperature ≥60℃)

### Bean Adaptation Table
| Bean Type | Water Temp | Grind | Flavor Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Ethiopia Yirgacheffe | 90℃ | Slightly coarse | Floral prominent, avoid high temp bitterness |
| Colombia Huila | 92℃ | Standard | Nutty chocolate tones |
| Kenya AA | 93℃ | Slightly fine | Bright high acidity, needs full extraction |

### Complaint Response
> Q: "Why so sour?"
> A: "This is Kenya bean's characteristic bright acidity, refreshing like citrus. If you prefer mellow, recommend Colombia bean." (natural recommendation)

> **Cost Control**: Pour-over bean loss rate ≤3% (excess needs explanation).
